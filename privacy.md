# EveryTab — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 13 July 2026

EveryTab is a local-first browser extension. It does not collect, transmit,
sell, or share any personal data.

- **What we collect:** Nothing is sent to us or any third party. EveryTab has no
  servers, no analytics, and no telemetry.
- **Where your data lives:** Your tabs, sessions, labels, and settings are stored
  locally in your browser and, if you turn it on, in a folder you choose on your
  own device (synced by your own file-sync tool). We never see it.
- **Permissions:** EveryTab uses browser permissions (such as tabs and storage)
  only to manage and save your tabs on your device — not to read page content or
  track browsing for any external purpose.
- **Optional history permission:** If you grant it, history is used only locally
  to power search and suggestions; it is never uploaded.
