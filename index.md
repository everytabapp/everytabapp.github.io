# EveryTab

**Every tab, kept, organized, local.**

EveryTab is a free, local-first browser extension that replaces the whole stack
of single-purpose tab tools (session saver, suspender, snoozer, dedup) with one
calm, coherent tool. Every tab flows through a unified Inbox, nothing EveryTab
closes ever disappears, and your data is plain JSON in a folder you own.

## Why EveryTab

- **Never lose a tab.** Anything EveryTab closes is kept and searchable, so you
  can close tabs with confidence.
- **Calm focus.** One Inbox to triage: restore, snooze, label, or file. No
  noise, no clutter.
- **Private by design.** No account, no server, no telemetry. Everything stays
  local, and syncs across your devices through a folder you control.
- **Free, with 18 themes.** Light and dark, 18 color schemes, and it stays free.
